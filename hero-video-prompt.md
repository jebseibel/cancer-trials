# Hero Background Video — AI Generation Prompt

**Concept:** Gentle cellular/molecular patterns, abstracted (not literal medical imagery) — nods to research and science without looking like a stock hospital photo. References breast cancer research thematically without literal anatomy.

## Main Prompt

Abstract macro visualization of translucent cellular structures slowly drifting and morphing in soft three-dimensional space, inspired by breast cancer research imagery but fully abstracted — no literal anatomy, no medical equipment. Organic spherical and honeycomb-like forms in soft coral pink, warm rose, and pale teal, glowing gently from within like bioluminescence. Structures pulse and drift at a very slow, meditative pace, occasionally merging and separating like cells dividing. Shallow depth of field with soft bokeh in the background. Light rays filter through the translucent forms creating a sense of hope and forward motion. Smooth, continuous camera drift — no cuts. Minimalist, elegant, calming — evokes scientific precision and biological research without appearing clinical or sterile. Color palette: dusty rose, soft coral, warm cream, muted teal accents. Seamless loop. 4K, soft focus, cinematic lighting, gentle ambient motion only.

## Negative Prompt (if supported)

needles, hospital, medical equipment, text, logos, human figures, harsh lighting, fast motion, sharp cuts

## Tuning Notes

- **Color variations:** Try both a cooler teal-forward version and a warmer coral-forward version — mood shifts significantly depending on which dominates.
- **"Cells dividing" phrase:** A subtle nod to the biology without being graphic. Drop it if you want something more purely abstract.
- **Clip length:** Most tools (Runway, Sora, Pika) cap generations at 5–10 seconds. Generate one clean, loopable segment, then loop it in a video editor rather than relying on native duration.
