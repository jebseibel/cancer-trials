# Logo generation prompt

Prompt to use:

A minimalist, warm, hand-crafted icon-style logo mark for a breast cancer clinical trial
finder app. A single stylized botanical or abstract symbol — a sprig of leaves, a simple
protective/shield shape formed from organic curves, or an abstract ribbon-adjacent form
(not a literal pink awareness ribbon) — rendered as a flat, geometric icon suitable for a
small navigation bar. Warm sage-to-deep-green color palette (deep forest green #3f5a3d as
the primary line/fill color, soft sage green #8fa888 as a secondary accent), on a
transparent or warm cream background (#f2ead9). No gradients, no photorealism, no drop
shadows, no clinical/medical iconography (no stethoscopes, no lab flasks, no crosses), no
human figures or faces. The style should feel calm, hopeful, and human rather than
corporate-medical or "startup dashboard" — think a small botanical woodcut or a modern
apothecary mark, not a tech-company glyph. Clean enough to read at 32px, single color or
two-tone only, no text/lettering in the mark itself.
